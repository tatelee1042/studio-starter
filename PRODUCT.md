# Product

## Register

brand

## Identity

Tate Lee is a business student focused on accounting with a double major in Japanese. He is seeking an internship and building this portfolio to support his outreach to recruiters.

## Users

The primary audience is internship recruiters who are likely to visit after Tate contacts them about an opportunity. They need to understand his background, assess his potential, and decide whether to invite him to interview.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio should establish immediate professional credibility and make Tate's qualifications easy to evaluate. Its goal is to leave recruiters thinking, "Wow. I need to hire this guy immediately," then give them a clear path toward an interview.

## Voice

Personable, professional, and friendly. The writing should feel confident and approachable without becoming boastful or overly casual.

## Work

Tate does not yet have portfolio projects. The Work page and one case-study page should remain honest templates until real work is available; the site must not present placeholder or imported projects as Tate's work.

## Pages

- Home
- Work
- One case-study template
- About

Thoughts, Consulting, and Now are excluded from the starting site map.

## Anti-references

The portfolio should not communicate personal opinions, thought leadership, lifestyle commentary, or unsupported claims. It should not imply experience, outcomes, or ownership that Tate cannot substantiate.

## Design Principles

1. Make Tate's internship intent immediately clear.
2. Lead with credible evidence and never invent experience.
3. Make recruiter evaluation fast and effortless.
4. Sound confident without becoming boastful.
5. Keep personal commentary out of the professional story.

## Success Criteria

A recruiter can understand who Tate is, what he studies, and that he is seeking an internship within ten seconds. The site gives the recruiter enough confidence and clarity to consider inviting him to interview.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA. Use semantic structure, full keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive alt text, sufficient color contrast, and reduced-motion support.
